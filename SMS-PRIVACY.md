# Grizz Line Messaging Program — Terms & Privacy Policy

**Program:** Grizz Line text messaging program

## Program description

Richard Garrison, an Onboarding Coordinator with Morgan Auto Group, uses the Grizz Line to send one-to-one text messages to individual job candidates and new hires about their own application and onboarding: welcome messages, reminders about required onboarding documents, orientation reminders, and first-day logistics. Messages are sent individually by the coordinator. This program sends no marketing, no advertising, and no bulk messages.

## How to opt in

You may opt in to this messaging program in either of two ways:

1. **During the hiring process.** Provide your mobile phone number on your Morgan Auto Group job application or onboarding paperwork and agree to be contacted by text message about your application and onboarding.
2. **By text message.** Text **START** to **+1 (352) 531-2029**.

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring onboarding text messages from Richard Garrison at the mobile number you provided.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your onboarding activity — typically a few messages per week while your onboarding is active, and none once it is complete.

## Costs

**Message and data rates may apply.** Charges are billed by, and payable to, your mobile service provider. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## How to opt out

Reply **STOP** to any message, at any time, to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message, and then no further messages unless you opt in again by texting **START** to +1 (352) 531-2029.

## How to get help

Reply **HELP** to any message, call **+1 (352) 531-2029**, or email **garrison.richard@gmail.com**.

## Privacy policy

**Information collected.** When you opt in, we collect your name, your mobile phone number, and the content of messages exchanged with the program.

**How information is used.** Your information is used only to send and manage the onboarding messages described above and to maintain records of consent.

**No sharing of mobile information.** No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

**Retention.** Opt-in records and message history are kept only as long as needed for the onboarding process and to demonstrate consent, and are then deleted.

**Your choices.** You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, as described above. To ask what information is held about you or to request its deletion, email garrison.richard@gmail.com.
